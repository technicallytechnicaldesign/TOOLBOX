Checklists

Reusable review checklists for technical drawings. Each opens as a live sign-off sheet — toggle the processes your drawing uses (sheet metal, welding, machining, casting…) so only relevant items show, tap an item to cycle OK → issue → N/A, watch the meter fill, and flag issues with a note. State saves in your browser and prints clean. Or build your own in the Builder.

Drawing Checklist

The full 2D-drawing review: title block, views, dimensions & tolerances, annotations, standards — plus toggle-in sections for sheet metal, welding, machining and casting. Also the starter template.

Joints, weld symbols and sizing, fit-up, distortion and sequence, inspection and post-weld finish — with toggle-in sections for carbon steel, stainless, aluminium, galvanised and code-governed work.

Sheet-Metal Flat

Build Your Own

Builder

Your saved checklists

Two ways to make one Quick: open the Builder, compose it with a live preview, hit Save — it lands in Your saved checklists above and opens from any browser you saved it in.

Permanent (in the repo): either Export HTML from the Builder, or copy template.html to checklists/<name>.html and edit its CHECKLIST data block. Drop the file in checklists/, add a tile here, commit. Skin (checklist.css), engine (checklist.js), tri-state items, process toggles, progress meter and print all come along unchanged. No build step, no framework.
