Hype Goblin

An ambient engineering familiar. He has opinions about your tolerances, your feature tree and your decision to make it one more configuration. None of the opinions are formally reviewed, none of the calculations are real, and no part of this page should be cited in a design review. Ask him something with the buttons, or leave him alone and he will start talking anyway.

Familiar: awake Advice quality: not assessed
You remind me of the babe!
✦✧✦
What this is Not a tool. Everything else in TOOLBOX computes something you can check. This one does not. It is a random line generator wearing a face, kept here because the rest of the workbench is load-bearing and something on the shelf should not be. He mutters on his own every 20 to 50 seconds, occasionally screams a tolerance callout that would end a career, and very rarely says something that lands harder than intended.

Destroy the evidence destroys nothing. It is a button that makes him laugh.
